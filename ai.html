Q1 What is Artificial Intelligence? ▾
A) A type of hardware processor
B) Simulation of human intelligence by machines
C) A programming language
D) A database management system
ANSWER: B
Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn like humans.
Q2 Who is considered the father of Artificial Intelligence? ▾
A) Alan Turing
B) John McCarthy
C) Marvin Minsky
D) Arthur Samuel
ANSWER: B
John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" in 1956 and is widely regarded as the father of AI.
Q3 Which of the following is NOT a subfield of AI? ▾
A) Machine Learning
B) Natural Language Processing
C) Compiler Design
D) Computer Networks
ANSWER: D
Computer Networks is a field of computer science but is not a subfield of AI. ML, NLP, Computer Vision, and Robotics are core AI subfields.

Q1 Define Artificial Intelligence and list its main goals. ▾
ANSWER
Artificial Intelligence is the branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. The main goals of AI include:

1. Reasoning — ability to solve problems logically.
2. Knowledge Representation — storing information about the world.
3. Planning — setting and achieving goals.
4. Learning — improving performance through experience.
5. Natural Language Processing — understanding and generating human language.
6. Perception — understanding the environment through sensors.
Q2 What is the Turing Test? ▾
ANSWER
The Turing Test, proposed by Alan Turing in 1950, is a test of a machine's ability to exhibit intelligent behaviour equivalent to, or indistinguishable from, that of a human. A human evaluator judges natural language conversations between a human and a machine. If the evaluator cannot reliably tell which is which, the machine is said to have passed the test.

Q1 Explain the different types of Artificial Intelligence with suitable examples. ▾
ANSWER
AI can be categorised into types based on capabilities and functionality:

Based on Capabilities:
1. Narrow AI (Weak AI) — Designed to perform a specific task. Examples: Siri, Alexa, chess-playing AI.
2. General AI (Strong AI) — Can perform any intellectual task like a human. Still theoretical.
3. Super AI — Surpasses human intelligence in all aspects. Hypothetical.

Based on Functionality:
1. Reactive Machines — No memory, only reacts to current input. Example: Deep Blue.
2. Limited Memory — Uses past experiences. Example: Self-driving cars.
3. Theory of Mind — Understands emotions and beliefs. Still in research.
4. Self-aware AI — Has consciousness. Hypothetical.
Q2 Discuss the applications of Artificial Intelligence in various domains. ▾
ANSWER
Applications of AI:

1. Healthcare: Disease diagnosis, drug discovery, personalised medicine, robotic surgery.
2. Finance: Fraud detection, algorithmic trading, credit scoring, risk management.
3. Transportation: Self-driving cars, traffic prediction, route optimisation.
4. Education: Personalised learning, intelligent tutoring systems, automated grading.
5. Entertainment: Recommendation systems (Netflix, YouTube), game AI, content generation.
6. Manufacturing: Predictive maintenance, quality control, robotic automation.
7. Agriculture: Crop monitoring, yield prediction, precision farming.
8. Customer Service: Chatbots, virtual assistants, sentiment analysis.
